**In**: The complete site on a preview deploy.

**Do**: Run the full `docs/INSPECTION_CHECKLIST.md` end to end. Test on a real phone, a slow
connection, and with a screen reader. Try to break the forms deliberately (Stage 02's Thief hat,
revisited).

**Out**: a filled-in, dated copy of `docs/INSPECTION_CHECKLIST.md`, with evidence for every row.

**Done when**: every row is PASS, or is GROWING/MISSING with Vaida's written sign-off and a fix
date.

**Do not look at**: new features. This stage finds problems, it does not add scope.
