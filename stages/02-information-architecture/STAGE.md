# Stage 02 — Information architecture

**In**: `docs/SITEMAP.md` from Stage 01.

**Do**: For each page, define its sections top to bottom in plain language (not layout, not
code) — what the visitor sees first, second, third, and what they're invited to do at each point.

**Out**: `docs/PAGE_BLUEPRINTS.md` — one short section per page.

**Done when**: someone could read the blueprint aloud and picture the page, with no design
software involved.

**Do not look at**: colours, fonts, components. This stage is structure only.
