## The Power Of Enough — Why More Will Never Be Enough Until You Feel Enough Within

There is a frozen bucket of water in a dark kitchen. A pair of borrowed boots, chosen for how they looked, not for the snow they were walking into. A stolen apple, and a grandmother's voice saying something about money that a small girl would carry for the rest of her life.

There is a £25 gift card, handed over after months of driving in pain, and a silence afterwards that said more than the card ever could.

These are not the whole story. They are the beginning of it.

Vaida's second book asks a harder question than her first: not why we don't have enough, but why so many successful, capable women never quite feel it, no matter how much they achieve. It is the book behind everything you've read on this site, where it actually started.
