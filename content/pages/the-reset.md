You already know something needs to change. What you are looking for is the courage to begin.

This is not a six-week plan to fix your finances. It is six weeks to rebuild the inner steadiness that makes every other change possible, so the financial clarity, the boundaries, the confidence, all of it, can finally hold.

The Always ENOUGH™ Emotional & Financial Reset is a six-week, one-to-one programme built around one belief: "your relationship with money is a relationship with yourself first."

We work together, week by week, through the Always E.N.O.U.G.H. Method, releasing old beliefs, rebuilding inner courage, and only then, once that foundation is steady, putting a calm, workable financial structure underneath it. Not the other way round.

This is not generic coaching, and it is not a course you work through alone. It is one-to-one, personally shaped around your actual life, your actual history, and your actual next step.

No two journeys through the Reset look identical. What follows is the real shape of the six weeks, the order this work happens in, though what fills each week is built around the person in front of me, one petal opening at a time, exactly when it's ready to.

**Week 1 — Understand.** A full, honest look at where you actually stand, financially and emotionally. No judgement, no overwhelm, just real clarity.

**Week 2 — Release.** Identifying the inherited beliefs and the inner critic driving your decisions, and starting to separate your own voice from what was handed to you.

**Week 3 — Organise.** Building a simple system for the everyday reality of your money, one that works for how you actually live.

**Week 4 — Activate.** Taking the first real, practical steps forward, specific to your situation, not a template.

**Week 5 — Strengthen.** Practising the new pattern in real situations, including the harder ones, boundaries, decisions, moments that used to feel impossible.

**Week 6 — Root.** Consolidating what's changed into something that holds, a plan you can keep using long after our six weeks together end.

Able to hold a boundary they couldn't hold six weeks earlier. Able to look at a bank statement without their chest tightening.
