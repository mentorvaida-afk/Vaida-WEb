## Section 1 — Opening

Emotional wellbeing and financial confidence are not two separate conversations. They never were.

I speak to organisations, schools, and audiences who are ready to understand that connection, and to act on it. Drawing on over thirty years designing learning that creates real behavioural change, I bring the same method that underpins the Always ENOUGH™ programme to keynotes, workshops, and training, adapted precisely to the room I'm speaking to.

## Section 2 — The Unifying Philosophy

Every audience I speak to is different. The core message is not.

Confidence, financial wellbeing, and how we navigate change, including the changes AI is bringing to how we work, are connected, not separate challenges to be solved one at a time. Whether I'm speaking to HR leaders, financial advisers, or a room of women at a midlife conference, the throughline is the same: real, lasting change starts on the inside, and the outer results follow.

What changes from room to room is the language, the examples, and the depth. What doesn't change is the substance behind it.

## Section 3 — Who I Speak For

**Corporate & HR — Emotional & Financial Wellbeing for Staff and Leadership**

Financial stress is one of the most consistently underestimated drivers of workplace disengagement, and one of the least openly discussed. I work with HR and leadership teams to bring emotional and financial wellbeing into the open, through keynotes, workshops, and structured training, built on the same behaviour-change design principles I've used across thirty years in education, regulated financial advice, and coaching. This is not generic wellbeing content. It is a learning experience, designed.

**AI Confidence Training — For Midlife & Non-Technical Professionals**

Roughly six in ten adults over fifty report real worry about AI, and only around a quarter describe themselves as familiar with the tools already reshaping their industries. I design and deliver confidence-first AI training specifically for non-technical professionals and teams, moving people from avoidance to genuine, working use within a single session or short structured programme. No jargon. No condescension. Just a clear, calm path from fear to fluency.

**Women's Empowerment, Confidence & Midlife Events**

This is where my own story and my professional work meet most directly. I speak on confidence, money, identity, and the particular experience of midlife reinvention, drawing on lived experience as much as expertise. For conferences, retreats, and women's events built around genuine transformation, not performative motivation.

**Financial Services & Adviser Networks**

Financial advisers are trained thoroughly in products, regulation, and process. Rarely are they trained in something just as important to the client relationship: how to recognise a client's money mindset, spending archetype, or the emotional pattern sitting underneath their financial decisions. That gap isn't a training failure, it's simply not what advisers are taught to look for, and closing it measurably deepens client trust and relationships.

Having worked for years as an FCA-regulated Protection Adviser myself, I understand this world from the inside. I deliver sessions for advisers and financial services teams on the emotional and behavioural dimension of client conversations, the side product training doesn't cover.

And here is something worth saying plainly: being good at advising others on money does not automatically mean being at peace with your own. I once worked with a financial adviser carrying significant personal debt, a highly capable professional by every external measure, who, through the programme, made real, lasting progress. There is no shame in that. Most of us, especially those raised in households, and countries, where money simply wasn't discussed, were never taught how to manage it. Advisers are no exception.

Available in person or online, with international travel welcomed for the right engagement.
