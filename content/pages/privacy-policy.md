## Section 2: What Information Is Collected

Depending on how you use this site, the following information may be collected:

- Contact forms (general enquiry, Speaking enquiry): name, email address, phone number (if provided), and any information you choose to share in your message.
- Free resource downloads: name and email address.
- Book launch notifications: email address only.
- Booking a Clarity Call: handled through Calendly, which has its own privacy policy governing that booking process.

No payment or financial information is collected directly through this website.

## Section 3: Why This Information Is Collected

Information submitted through this site is used to:

- Respond to your enquiry or question
- Send you the free resource you've requested
- Notify you when The Power Of Enough is published, if you've asked to be notified
- Occasionally send relevant updates, articles, or offers by email, if you've opted in to this

Your information is never sold to third parties.

## Section 4: How Your Information Is Stored

Form submissions are stored securely and may be processed using the following third-party tools:

- Next.js server functions, the website's own form-handling code, which passes submissions directly to the tools listed below. No third-party form plugin is used.
- Google Sheets, for internal record-keeping of enquiries
- SendPulse, for managing email communications you've opted into
- Calendly, for scheduling Clarity Calls

Your information is kept only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected
for, or as required by law.

## Section 5: Cookies

This site may use cookies to understand how visitors use it and to improve your experience. You
can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. This section should be expanded
once the specific analytics/tracking tools used on the live site are confirmed.

## Section 6: Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

- Ask what personal information is held about you
- Ask for it to be corrected or deleted
- Withdraw consent to marketing emails at any time (every email includes an unsubscribe link)
- Make a complaint to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you have concerns about how your data is handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@alwaysenoughmethod.com.
